A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before the first pull.

How does a live break work?+

You choose an available break or packs, complete the purchase steps, and Mike opens the packs live on YouTube. Wheel entries have their own entry link and instructions on the product.

How do I pay?+

The checkout walks you through the available payment method. With Streamlabs, reserve first, then copy your order reference and break name into the tip message with your YouTube username.

What about shipping and order limits?+

Shipping details appear on each break. Pack quantity limits and available stock are shown before you reserve. Pack purchases allow one order per email for each item.

Where can I ask Mike a question?+

Come say hello on Pokeballer Mike’s YouTube channel . Keep your order reference handy when asking about a purchase.
